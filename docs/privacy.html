PRIVACY POLICY

YOUR CALLEffective 1 Oct 2026

Talkies works without an account. Without one, Talkies does not collect personal data and your diary stays on your phone. An account is optional. If you create one, the Talkies server stores the data listed on this page. Talkies has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, with or without an account.

What stays on your phone

Your stubs, ratings, notes, tags, places, seats, prices, watchlist, settings and the poster photos you choose for your own films. Talkies keeps them in its private storage on the device. Without an account, we cannot see them and we do not receive a copy.

With an account, Talkies sends a copy of your diary to the Talkies server. Talkies does not send your settings or your poster photos. See the section Your account.

Your phone's own backup (iCloud Backup or Android backup) can include this data. That backup goes to your Apple or Google account under their terms, not to us.

Network requests

To show posters, synopses, streaming services and new releases, Talkies sends requests to Wikipedia and Wikidata, which the Wikimedia Foundation runs. A request contains a film title or a Wikidata ID. It does not contain your diary, your ratings or any ID that identifies you.

Like every web server, Wikimedia receives your IP address and basic device details with each request. See the Wikimedia privacy policy. Talkies caches images and text on the phone so that it asks less often.

Some versions of Talkies can connect to a Talkies server for the optional account. A version without a server address never contacts a Talkies server.

A version that has a server address sends a status check to the server when you open Settings. The check contains no identifier and no diary data. Like every web server, the Talkies server receives your IP address and basic device details with each request. Without an account, the status check is the only request that goes to the Talkies server.

Photos and camera

When you add a film yourself, you can pick a photo as its poster. Talkies copies that one photo into its storage. It never uploads it, even if you have an account. Talkies asks for access only when you tap to pick a photo.

Files you export

CSV exports, JSON backups and share images go only where you send them with the system share sheet.

Your account

An account is optional. You can sign in with an email code, with Google or with Apple. Which options you see depends on your version of Talkies. Sign-in appears in Settings, under Account, when the app can reach the Talkies server.

With an account, the Talkies server stores the data below. The server operator is whoever runs that server.

  • Sign-in details: your email address, or the Google or Apple account that you use. We use your email address only to sign you in.
  • Your profile: handle, display name, stamp ink, who can see your profile (Private or Friends only) and whether you share ratings.
  • Your diary: each stub with all its details, such as the date, place, seat, price, notes and the people you went with. The diary also holds your watchlist with planned dates, your tags, your places and your hidden films. Your own films are stored without the poster photo.
  • A taste summary: weights for directors, actors, genres, film series, languages, eras and streaming services. Talkies builds it without your private stubs. It holds no list of films.
  • Friends: your friend requests, your friends, your blocks and your emoji reactions.
  • Groups: group names, member names, guest names that a group owner adds, the shared watchlist, the group deck and your swipes.
  • Movie nights: the films, the time slots, the place, your votes and your replies.
  • Messages: chat messages in your groups, and films that you send to friends with a note.
  • Reports: the reason, the note and, for a message, a copy of the message. When a report is removed, a moderation record of it stays: the type, the target, the reason, the note and the date, without your account id.

Reminders for movie nights are local notifications. Your phone schedules them. The Talkies server sends no push messages and stores no device tokens.

Who can see what

  • A new profile is Private. No other user can see your films.
  • If you choose Friends only, your friends see the films you watched, your top films, your watchlist and a few totals. Your friends see your ratings only if you allow it.
  • No one sees the dates in your diary. Friends never see your notes, places, seats, prices, tags or the people you went with.
  • A stub that you mark private is never shown to another user. It stays in your account, so your own phones can show it.
  • Another user who knows your exact handle can find you. They see your handle, display name and stamp ink. They can send you a friend request.
  • Group members see each other's names and handles, the shared watchlist, the chat, the poll results and the replies to movie nights.
  • The group deck comes from an anonymous mix of the members' watchlists and taste. The deck shows no names.
  • Anyone who has the invite link or code of a group can join it. The group owner can replace the code.
  • If you block a user, you cannot see each other anywhere in the app. Blocking also ends a friendship.
  • You can report a user, a message or a group. The server stores the report so that the server operator can review it.
  • The server operator and the hosting providers can access the data stored on the server. The server stores your diary in a form that it can read. It is not end-to-end encrypted.

Service providers

Supabase hosts the Talkies database and the sign-in service. The Talkies server runs at a hosting provider. The server operator chooses the hosting provider and the hosting region. This page does not name a region.

An email service sends your sign-in code. The server operator chooses that service.

Google or Apple takes part only if you sign in with it. That sign-in follows the privacy policy of Google or Apple. Talkies does not receive your Google or Apple password.

Keep and delete your data

Without an account, Talkies keeps no data on a server. With an account, the Talkies server keeps your data until you delete it or you delete your account. Backups made by the hosting provider can keep deleted data for a limited time.

On your phone, tear up a stub to delete it, or uninstall the app to delete everything on the phone. If you are signed in, tearing up a stub also removes it from your account at the next sync. Signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

To delete your account, open Settings, then Account, then Delete account, and confirm. This removes your profile, your diary and the rest of your data from the Talkies server. One exception: reports you made or received lose your account id but keep their reason, note, date and target, so the server operator keeps a moderation record. The diary on your phone stays.

If you cannot open the app, read Delete your account. It explains how to ask for deletion outside the app.

Children

Talkies is not directed at children under 13. The optional account is not for them. Without an account, Talkies collects no data from anyone, of any age.

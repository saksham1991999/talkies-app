WHAT IS TALKIES?

DIARYA stub for every watch

Talkies is a movie ticket diary for India. Every film you watch becomes a ticket stub, printed on coloured paper like an old single-screen hall ticket.

Each stub holds the film, the date, the hall, the seat, the show and the price. Rewatches get their own stubs, and the collection becomes your year in films.

Talkies is free, with no ads and no tracking. It needs no account, works offline with a 34,000-title catalogue on your phone, and speaks 8 Indian languages.

EVERY WATCH, A STUB

STUBHall details

Log the show (morning, matinee, evening or night), the class (Balcony, Dress Circle, Box, Recliner, Gold and more), the seat number, the price in rupees and the format: IMAX, 4DX, ScreenX, Dolby Cinema or 3D.

First day, first show gets an FDFS stamp. Watched a dubbed version? Save the language you watched it in. Do not remember the date? Save only the month or the year.

Rate in 0.1 steps, write notes and add unlimited tags. Add your own halls and single screens.

MADE FOR INDIAN CINEMA

DESIBollywood to Mollywood

Log Bollywood, Tollywood, Kollywood, Mollywood and Sandalwood films, plus Bengali, Marathi, Punjabi and Gujarati cinema, Indian web series and world cinema, in one diary.

Search ignores spaces and punctuation, finds films by actor or director, and works in Hindi, Tamil and other Indian scripts. See where a film streams, from Wikipedia and Wikidata. Cannot find a film? Add it yourself with your own poster photo.

YOUR YEAR IN FILMS

STATSCalendar and watchlist

Stats for any month, any year or all time: watch time, films per month, ratings, money spent on tickets and FDFS count. Top languages, genres, directors, actors, halls, formats and show times, where tapping any bar shows its films.

A calendar of posters marks every day you watched a film, and the watchlist holds what you plan to see, with planned dates. Share a ticket, a month or your stats as an image.

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

FAQEight answers

What is Talkies?

Talkies is a free movie ticket diary for India. Every film you watch becomes a ticket stub with the hall, seat, show and price, and your stubs add up to a year in films.

Is Talkies free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no ads and no tracking.

Do I need an account to use Talkies?

No. Talkies works without an account and your diary stays on your phone. Signing in is optional; it also syncs a copy of your diary to the Talkies server.

Does Talkies work offline?

Yes. A 34,000-title catalogue lives on your phone, so search and logging work without a connection. Posters, synopses and streaming info load when you are online.

Which languages does Talkies support?

Eight: English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Kannada and Malayalam, with dark mode too.

Is Talkies on Android?

Talkies is on iPhone and iPad now, and Android is coming soon. Email info@sakshammittal.com for early access.

Where does my diary live?

On your phone. Talkies has no analytics, no ads and no tracking. You can import and export CSV, and back up and restore everything as one file. Details are in the privacy policy.

How is Talkies different from other movie diary apps?

Talkies is built around the ticket stub: each viewing gets its own stub with the show, class, seat, price in rupees and an FDFS stamp for first day, first show. It is made for Indian cinema, works fully offline, and needs no account.

You can delete your Talkies account in the app. This removes your data from the Talkies server, except for the moderation records described below. The diary on your phone stays.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Open Account.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm.

The app deletes your account and signs you out. It keeps working without an account.

Delete account needs a connection to the Talkies server. If the app cannot reach the server, try again later.

What is removed

Deleting your account removes your data from the Talkies server. This includes:

  • Your profile and your sign-in details
  • Your synced diary and your taste summary
  • Your friends, friend requests, blocks and reactions
  • Your messages, swipes, votes and replies
  • Your group memberships

If you own a group, ownership passes to the member with an account who joined first. If no such member exists, the group is deleted.

The privacy policy lists the data that the server stores.

What stays

  • The diary on your phone. Deleting your account does not delete your stubs, ratings or watchlist on the phone. To delete them, tear up the stubs or uninstall the app.
  • Backups. The hosting provider can keep backups. A backup can hold deleted data for a limited time.
  • Stubs in other diaries. A movie night wrap-up can put your name on a stub in another member's diary. That stub belongs to the other member and stays.
  • Moderation records. When an account is deleted, the reports that it filed, or that point at it, are archived before they are removed. The archive holds the kind of report (a user, a message or a group), the id of the reported user, message or group, the reason, the note and the date. It holds no name, no email and no profile, and no copy of a reported message. A note is free text, so it can contain names or details that the reporter typed. For a report about a person, the id is that person's account id. The server operator keeps the archive to review reports.

If you cannot open the app

This page exists for app store policy. Deleting in the app is the fast path.

If you cannot open the app, email us and we will delete the account for you:

  1. Write to support@talkies.in from the email address on the account. A message from any other address cannot show that the account is yours.
  2. Use the subject Delete my account.
  3. Tell us the handle. Send it only by email, never in a public post.

Email goes to one person and is not public. To prove the account is yours, we send a one-time code to the address on the account and delete only after you send it back. We do not delete an account on a request alone.

If email does not work

As a fallback, you can open an issue at github.com/saksham1991999/talkies-app/issues with the title Delete my account.

GitHub issues are public. Do not post your email address, your handle or any personal data in the issue. We reply in the issue and move the rest to email. Deletion still needs the confirmation code sent to the address on the account.

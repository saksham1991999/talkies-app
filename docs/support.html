Where is my diary stored?

On your phone. Without an account, Talkies does not send your diary to any server. If you sign in, Talkies also syncs a copy of your diary to the Talkies server. The privacy policy lists what the server stores.

How do I move my diary to a new phone?

Use a backup file. Open Settings, then Your data, then Back up everything. Send the JSON file to yourself. On the new phone, open Settings, then Restore a backup, and choose the file.

Or use your account. Sign in on the new phone with the same account. Talkies copies your diary from the server to the new phone. Poster photos that you chose for your own films do not move with either method.

What does signing in give me?

Signing in is optional. It adds sync between your phones, friends, groups whose members use their own phones, and chat. Every core feature works without an account.

To sign in, open Settings, then Account. You can sign in with an email code, with Google or with Apple. Which options you see depends on your version. The Account section appears when the app can reach the Talkies server.

Who can see my films?

Only you, until you choose otherwise. A new profile is Private. If you choose Friends only, your friends see the films you watched. They see your ratings only if you allow it.

No one sees the dates in your diary. A stub that you mark private is never shown to another user. The privacy policy has the full list.

What happens when the server is down?

The app keeps working. Your diary, calendar, stats and watchlist are on your phone. Features that need the server are hidden until it is back. Changes that you make in the meantime sync when the server returns.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings, then Account, then Delete account, and confirm. This removes your profile, your diary and the rest of your data from the Talkies server. Reports keep their reason, note, date and target without your account id, so a moderation record stays. The diary on your phone stays.

If you cannot open the app, read Delete your account.

A film is missing

Open Settings and tap Refresh film list. If the film is still missing, search for it and add it yourself with your own poster photo.

Film details are wrong

Film details come from Wikidata and Wikipedia. On the film page, tap Correct these details to fix them at the source. The fix reaches Talkies at the next refresh.

Can I import from another app?

Yes. Settings, then Import CSV, reads Talkies and Letterboxd CSV files.
